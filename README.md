# Flappy Pilo

Meet Pilo, the little white star from CyCade. Pilo wears a tiny purple cap, and today Pilo is flying through space.

The trouble is, space is full of tall server racks blinking with cyan lights. Your job is to help Pilo slip through the gaps between them, and grab as many glowing Cysic C coins as you can along the way.

You can play it right now in your browser, no download needed:
https://flappy-pilo.vercel.app/

## How to play

On your phone, just tap the screen. Every tap gives Pilo a little flap upward. Stop tapping and Pilo starts to fall.

On a computer, press Space or click with your mouse. The up arrow, W and Enter work too, so use whatever feels comfortable.

That's really all there is to it. Short, gentle taps work better than frantic ones.

## How scoring works

Every time Pilo makes it through a gap, you get 10 points. Every Cysic coin you grab is worth 25, so it pays to go after them.

You start with 3 hearts. Bumping into a server rack, the floor or the top of the screen costs you one heart. Pilo blinks for a moment afterwards, and while blinking, Pilo can't get hurt again, so use that second to get back on track.

Each round lasts 90 seconds, and the gaps slowly get tighter as the clock runs down. The round ends when the time is up or you run out of hearts. Your best score is saved on your own device, so you always have something to beat next time.

## Run it yourself

The whole game lives in one small file. Download this project, double-click the game file, and it opens in your browser. There's nothing to install and nothing to set up.
